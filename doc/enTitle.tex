\en-abstract{
	 NoVazheh was developed to provide structured opportunities for vocabulary practice alongside everyday interaction between parents and children under three years of age. It is a supplementary educational application for presenting and reviewing Persian vocabulary through audiovisual content and interactive activities. Its purpose is to create brief opportunities for attending to a word and selecting a related image; it does not replace human interaction or specialist assessment of language development. The project implements vocabulary review and an auditory quiz, several educational games, and parent workflows for managing child profiles and custom content. Quiz questions prioritize words not yet marked as learned in the application's records. Parents can manage custom words with images and recorded audio, although these words are not automatically included in the standard learning and quiz flow. Selected games provide staged progression, and vocabulary records or progress in supported games can be associated with a child profile; activities do not share a single progression or persistence model. The application uses Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform for Android and iOS targets, with a Ktor backend providing account, content, and supported data services. Selected software paths received limited technical verification. No formal usability study or empirical evaluation of educational effectiveness was conducted. Accordingly, this work reports the implementation of an interactive supplementary tool and makes no claim that it improves language development or has clinical effectiveness.}
